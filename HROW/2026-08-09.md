## 2. Business and financials

Harrow is a commercial-stage specialty ophthalmic pharma. Key products: VEVYE (dry
eye disease), IHEEZO, TRIESENCE, plus a legacy portfolio (VIGAMOX, ILEVRO, FLAREX,
NATACYN, ZERVIATE, BYQLOVI). On Aug 3, 2026 the company signed an Asset Purchase
Agreement with Viatris to acquire global rights to TYRVAYA (varenicline nasal
spray, dry eye disease) for $30M cash at closing plus up to $70M in contingent
net-sales milestones; expected close H2 2026, no assurance of timing or completion
— [DISCLOSED], full text of the 8-K read.

**Growth and margin quality.** Revenue: FY2022 $88.6M → FY2023 $130.2M → FY2024
$199.6M → FY2025 $272.3M (annual CAGR ~46%). The quarterly trend is choppier than
the annual number suggests: Q1 2025 $47.8M → Q2 2025 $63.7M → Q3 2025 $71.6M →
(implied) Q4 2025 ~$89.2M → **Q1 2026 $44.2M**, a 7.6% year-over-year *decline* and
a large sequential drop. Management attributed most of it to an $8M one-time VEVYE
gross-to-net adjustment tied to new commercial coverage and co-pay support, while
pointing to record VEVYE prescriptions (surpassing Xiidra, ~14% dry-eye share) as
evidence the underlying business is fine — [REPORTED] via press coverage of the
print, not the primary release text itself.

Gross margin has historically run ~75% (FY2024 75.3%, FY2025 75.1%, Q3 2025 75.3%)
but fell to **61.2%** in Q1 2026 — the same quarter as the gross-to-net hit.
Whether that snaps back is the single most decision-relevant fact not yet known;
it resolves tomorrow.

Operating income turned positive and has grown every year: FY2022 $1.9M → FY2023
$0.4M → FY2024 $8.8M → FY2025 $30.5M. Net income has stayed negative throughout
(FY2025 net loss -$5.1M) because interest expense on the debt load (below) is
consuming most of that operating profit. FCF: FY2023 +$2.4M, FY2024 -$23.8M,
FY2025 +$43.0M — directionally recovering but too volatile to call a stable trend
yet.

**Balance sheet.** Cash $94.6M as of 2026-03-31, up from $72.9M at YE2025.
Long-term debt rose from $250.0M (2025-12-31) to $300.0M (2026-03-31) — the
composition of that $50M increase (revolver draw vs. other) was not retrieved this
session, a real gap. The core debt is $250M of senior unsecured notes at **8.625%
coupon, due September 15, 2030**, refinanced in Sept 2025 alongside a new $40M
revolving facility with Fifth Third Bank, replacing an Oaktree term loan. At 8.625%
on $250M, annual cash interest is roughly $21.6M before the revolver — most of
FY2025's entire $30.5M operating income. This is the mechanical reason net income
stays negative even as the operating business improves, and it leaves little room
for a revenue disappointment before the interest burden bites into liquidity. Net
debt (LT debt less cash, both as of 2026-03-31) ≈ $205.4M [C].

**Share count and dilution.** Diluted shares grew 27.46M (2022) → 36.76M (2025),
+33.9% cumulative. SBC as % of revenue has been improving: 12.1% (2023) → 8.8%
(2024) → 4.6% (2025) — a real positive, less dilution intensity as the company
scales.

revenue needed to justify today's $1,712M EV [C]:

| Terminal FCF margin | Exit multiple | Required FY2030 revenue | Implied 5-yr revenue CAGR |

| 25% | 18x | ~$671M | ~20% |

The middle case (~30% CAGR) is roughly what management's own guidance and Street

## 7. Bull case / bear case

**Bull** (the Street's case — 8/8 analysts rate Strong Buy, avg target $67.75,
range $59-$88): VEVYE is winning share in a real, durable way against an entrenched
incumbent; IHEEZO and TRIESENCE are growing fast (TRIESENCE demand +136% reported);
the sales force is being doubled across all three products; TYRVAYA adds a fourth,
differentiated dry-eye asset and diversifies away from single-product dependence;
operating income has been positive and growing for three straight years; SBC
intensity is falling, not rising; cash improved sequentially into Q1 2026. The Q1
miss is a disclosed, quantified, one-time gross-to-net item layered on top of
record prescription volume, not a demand problem.

**Bear** (steelman of what a competent short would argue): the Q1 2026 print
showed real cracks exactly where the bull case needs strength — revenue declined
year-over-year and gross margin fell 14 points in a single quarter, and "one-time"
is an assertion, not yet a fact (resolves tomorrow). The company just took on $250M
of 8.625% debt that consumes essentially all of FY2025's hard-won operating profit,
leaving little cushion if Q2 disappoints again. Consensus embeds a near-6x jump in
EPS for FY2027 requiring flawless integration of an unclosed acquisition plus a
margin inflection that hasn't shown up yet. The stock already trades at a real
premium (5.6x P/S) to comparable profitable specialty-pharma peers (COLL 1.2x,
SUPN 3.3x) even after adjusting for faster growth. Short interest is ~18% of shares
outstanding with days-to-cover rising, not falling. And this isn't the company's
first credibility episode — a 2023 short report alleged concealment of a DOJ
investigation and an FDA warning letter, resolution unconfirmed.

Neither side is fully rebutted: the bear case's central claim (margin didn't hold
in Q1) is a fact, not an inference. The bull case's core claim (VEVYE share gains
are real) is also independently sourced. Tomorrow's print is genuinely the swing
factor — consistent with the options market charging 76% IV for it.

## 9. Risks and frictions

**Asymmetry test:** (Base $49 − spot $40.44) / (spot $40.44 − Bear $28) = 8.56 /
12.44 = **0.69 — below the 2:1 threshold.** Only using the 25%-probability Bull
case as the reference point does the ratio clear 2:1 (2.46), which is not the

favorably at the base case, even though the probability-weighted expected value
sits modestly above spot — the expected-value figure is pulled up by a lower-
probability bull case with larger magnitude, not by the balance of likely outcomes.

**Liquidity:** ~$13M/day traded — adequate for a $1.5B-cap name, not deep; a name
with 76% IV and ~18%-of-shares-outstanding short interest can move violently on
modest volume.

**Event risk:** maximal right now — earnings land tomorrow, before this report is
even a day old.

**Leverage:** $250M of 8.625% fixed-rate notes due 2030 plus an unclarified
additional $50M of debt taken on between YE2025 and Q1 2026 means annual cash
interest runs close to all of FY2025's operating profit — a real, structural
constraint on how much revenue disappointment the balance sheet can absorb before
liquidity becomes the story instead of growth.

**Short side:** borrow fee and hard-to-borrow status not retrieved this session;
at ~18% of shares outstanding already short with rising days-to-cover, this is not
a name to assume cheap, available borrow on, and squeeze risk (either direction
around tomorrow's print) is real.

**Concentration:** single-sector (ophthalmology) revenue base; VEVYE, IHEEZO, and
TRIESENCE collectively carry most of the near-term growth story.

Frictions: wash-sale rules, short- vs. long-term capital-gains treatment, and
options-assignment mechanics all vary by jurisdiction — confirm locally before
acting.
